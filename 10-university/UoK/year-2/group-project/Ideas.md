### FixMate – Home Repair Service Booking Platform

FixMate is a website that connects customers with trusted technicians for home repair services such as plumbing, electrical repairs, AC repairs, painting, and appliance repairs.
Customers can find technicians, check their details, book services, track booking status, and give ratings and reviews.
Technicians can register, manage their services, and accept customer bookings.


Main Goal: To make finding and booking reliable home repair services easier and faster.

### AI-Based Smart Traffic Light Control

Concept: Traffic lights automatically adjust their timing based on vehicle density.

Features:
- Detect vehicles using cameras.
- Calculate traffic density.
- Dynamically change signal timing.
- Prioritize emergency vehicles.
- Traffic analytics dashboard.
- Technology: Python, YOLO, OpenCV, Arduino.

### SafeGuard SL 2.0

A trilingual scam-message detector with a community reporting database. It would have:
1. **A dataset**, built by collecting and labelling Sri Lankan scam and legitimate messages (this is the Data Collection chapter).
2. **An ML classifier** (TF-IDF with logistic regression, then maybe a small multilingual model), evaluated with precision and recall and compared against your old keyword method.
3. **A backend API** for the model and the report database.
4. **An Android app.** Users paste or share a message to it instead of granting SMS access, which avoids the permission problem.
5. **A web dashboard** for community reports of scam numbers and URLs, with moderation.
6. **A user study** with real people, including the older adults you claim to target.
