
- `:!q` - quit without saving
- `:x` - save and exit
- `w` - jump to the next word
---
- `a` or `i` - insert mod
- `Shift + a` or `A` - insert mode but it moves to the end of the current line
- `Shift + i` or `I` - insert mode but it moves to the begin of the current line
---
- `u` - Undo
- `Ctrl + r` - Redo
