##### *JFrame* close options
- EIXT_ON_CLOSE - completes stops the jvm
- HIDE_ON_CLOSE - makes the *JFrame* invisible (default)
- DISPOSE_ON_CLOSE - closes the current *JFrame*
- DO_NOTHING_ON_CLOSE - does nothing, skips the action

`System.exit(0)` - Immediate jvm termination
